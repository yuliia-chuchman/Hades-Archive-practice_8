The World of Ancient Egypt

Ancient Egyptian mythology includes stories about creation, the gods, the journey of the sun and the afterlife. It was closely connected with religion, royal power and everyday life.

The Ancient Gods

Sun god who travelled across the sky each day.

Anubis

God associated with mummification and funerary traditions.

Isis

Goddess associated with magic, protection and motherhood.
