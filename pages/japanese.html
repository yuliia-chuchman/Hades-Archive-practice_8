JAPANESE MYTHOLOGY

Discover a world where nature, spirits and legends are deeply connected.

The World of Japanese Myths

Japanese mythology includes ancient creation stories, nature spirits and supernatural beings. Many of these traditions are connected with Shinto beliefs and Japanese folklore.

Important Deities

Amaterasu

Goddess of the sun and one of the most important Shinto deities.

Susanoo

Deity associated with storms and the sea.

Tsukuyomi

Deity associated with the moon.

Mysterious Creatures

Kitsune

Fox spirits known for intelligence and supernatural abilities.

Tengu

Supernatural beings often connected with mountains and forests.
