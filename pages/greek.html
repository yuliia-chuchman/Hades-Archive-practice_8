The World of Ancient Greece

Ancient Greek mythology is a collection of myths about gods, heroes, monsters and the origins of the world. These stories influenced art, literature and culture for thousands of years.

The Olympian Gods

Zeus

King of the gods, ruler of the sky and wielder of lightning.

Athena

Goddess of wisdom, strategic warfare and crafts.

Hades

God of the underworld and ruler of the realm of the dead.

Famous Mythical Creatures

Medusa

A Gorgon whose gaze was said to turn people into stone.

Cerberus

The three-headed dog guarding the entrance to the underworld.
